From spreadsheet to defensible analysis

Excel

Preserved the original pivots and charts, then added a corrected formula-driven workbook. Distinct-order denominators and canceled-order treatment are explicit.

SQL

Typed order-line table, order-level view, validation queries, CTEs, monthly LAG and market-level DENSE_RANK. Results reconcile with an independent calculation.

Power BI

Two fact tables sharing date, mode and market dimensions, DAX measures and three report pages. Native Desktop refresh/render verification is pending.

Business interpretation

Review service promises and scheduling assumptions for the highest-rate mode, then investigate the highest-volume source of late orders. These are investigation priorities, not proof of carrier causality or measured savings.
